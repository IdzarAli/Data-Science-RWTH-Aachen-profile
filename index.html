Data Science RWTH Aachen

Welcome to the Data Science program at RWTH Aachen University! Our program offers a comprehensive curriculum that combines theoretical knowledge with practical applications in the field of data science. Join us to explore the world of data and unlock new opportunities in this rapidly growing field.

Program Overview

Machine Learning

Learn how machines can identify patterns and make predictions from data. Explore supervised and unsupervised learning, model evaluation, and feature engineering.

Statistics

Build a strong foundation in probability, statistical inference, regression, and data analysis to support data-driven decision making.

Artificial Intelligence

Explore intelligent systems and modern AI techniques, including deep learning, neural networks, and intelligent decision-making systems.

Data Engineering

Learn how to collect, process, manage, and organize large datasets to build reliable data pipelines and support analytical applications.
